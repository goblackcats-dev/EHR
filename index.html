NursingSim Case Builder

Faculty tool for generating internally consistent simulated patients for NursingSim EHR.

1. Patient Skeleton

Provide the small amount of information you want to control. Diagnosis profiles will fill in typical defaults.

Medical History Select all diagnoses that should shape the patient.
Diagnosis profiles are educational defaults, not hard clinical rules. For example, hypertension may contribute lisinopril or metoprolol and a higher BP trend, but you can edit any generated item before export.

2. Diagnosis Effects

See exactly what each selected diagnosis is contributing before the case is built.

Choose a primary problem and/or medical history diagnoses.

3. Generated Case

Review the generated clinical content, make edits, then export canonical v2 JSON.
