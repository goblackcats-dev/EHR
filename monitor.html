NBP trend

Time

Follow the EHR: open the EHR in another tab of this same browser. Pressing 4h ▶ there moves this monitor too.

Vitals now

"Apply" changes the numbers and waves at once. The blood pressure changes only when the cuff cycles (like a real monitor): use Measure BP, or it cycles by itself at each new time step.

Sound and alarms

Alarm limits: HR below 50 or above 120, SpO2 below 92, systolic below 90 or above 180, RR below 10 or above 24, temp above 100.4. The sound only works after you click the button above once.

Scenario (times and vitals)

One row per time: the monitor shows the latest row at or before the clock. Edit it, then press Save. It is kept in this browser.
Fields per row: t, hr, spo2, sys, dia, rr, temp, rhythm (sinus, brady, avb1, widebrady), pr, qrs, tAmp, o2 (text), note. Keys: C = this panel, N = measure BP, F = full screen, S = sound.
