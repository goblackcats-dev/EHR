Time

Follow the EHR: open the EHR in another tab of this same browser. Pressing 4h ▶ there moves this monitor too.

Vitals now

"Apply" changes the numbers and waves at once. The blood pressure changes only when the cuff cycles (like a real monitor): use Measure BP, or it cycles by itself at each new time step.

Review and artifact

Click the ECG wave (or press R) to review the last 4 patient-hours: a heart-rate trend, a list of events (PVCs, runs of V-tach, slow rates, artifact) and a 12-second strip for any moment you pick. To plan exact events for a case, add "events": [{"at":"2026-11-17 13:42","type":"nsvt","beats":6,"rate":170}] to a scenario row (types: pvc, couplet, nsvt, artifact).

Sound and alarms

Alarm limits: HR below 50 or above 120, SpO2 below 92, systolic below 90 or above 180, RR below 10 or above 24, temp above 100.4. The sound only works after you click the button above once.

Scenario (times and vitals)

One row per time: the monitor shows the latest row at or before the clock. Edit it, then press Save. It is kept in this browser for this patient only (the EHR tells the monitor which patient is open; built-in scenarios: Raymond Kessler SIM-40318, Lauren Mitchell SIM-40422, DKA series SIM-40501 and up).
Natural variation: every 2-3 seconds the numbers drift a little (HR ±3, SpO2 ±1, RR ±1) and each blood pressure reading differs a little (±5 / ±3), never across an alarm limit. A row can set its own ranges, for example "var": {"hr": 2, "sys": 3}. The cuff takes 45 seconds, with pump and valve sounds when sound is on. Numbers you type yourself (Apply, or the EHR Monitor window) are used exactly. Fields per row: t, hr, spo2, sys, dia, rr, temp, rhythm (sinus, brady, tachy, avb1, widebrady), pr, qrs, tAmp, uAmp (U wave, low potassium, e.g. 0.1), pvc (optional: premature ventricular beats per minute drawn on the live ECG, e.g. 6; leave it out for none), o2 (text), note. Keys: C = this panel, N = measure BP, F = full screen, S = sound.
