# Denise Carter — simulated adult DKA patient chart and instructor design

**FICTIONAL EDUCATIONAL CASE.** All patient details, staff, organizations, transactions, appointments, and clinical events below are invented. Real manufacturer programs are linked as educational resources; simulated approvals, prices, supplies, and appointments are not actual arrangements. This document contains an instructor timeline and the simulated chart. Release each result or note only at its stated availability time.

**Time convention:** Central Standard Time, 24-hour clock. Day 1 = Monday, November 9, 2026; Day 2 = November 10; Day 3 = November 11; Day 4 = November 12. ED arrival is during the overnight period immediately before Day 1's 0700 unit arrival. Simulation duration is exactly Day 1 0700–Day 4 0700: six 12-hour shifts and seven boundary checkpoints. Times without a day label in a table inherit that table's day.

**Consensus reconciliation:** The requested “glucose <250 plus two criteria” checklist is not the 2024 consensus definition. The consensus requires beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB) <0.6 mmol/L **and at least one of** venous pH ≥7.3 or bicarbonate ≥18 mmol/L; ideally glucose is also <200 mg/dL. Anion-gap closure alone is insufficient. This case meets the actual definition and all four elements of the requested checklist before transition. Initial insulin is 0.1 unit/kg/hour without a bolus; when glucose falls below 250, dextrose is added and insulin decreases to 0.05 unit/kg/hour. Basal insulin overlaps the infusion for two hours. These choices follow the [2024 adult hyperglycemic-crises consensus](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00125-024-06183-8).

**Instructor separation:** Only Section 2 contains recorded vital signs, ending at the Day 1 0700 arrival. All later numeric physiologic settings appear exclusively in Section 7 as simulator programming, not charted observations. Subsequent notes deliberately contain no numeric vital signs. Glucose measurements and ECG interpretations are separate diagnostic data.

**Calculation audit:**

- **Anion gap = measured Na − (Cl + HCO₃).** Chronological calculations for all 13 rows: 130−(94+9)=27; 133−(100+12)=21; 135−(103+13)=19; 136−(105+14)=17; 137−(107+14)=16; 137−(108+16)=13; 138−(108+20)=10; 138−(107+22)=9; 138−(105+24)=9; 139−(105+25)=9; 139−(105+25)=9; 139−(104+26)=9; 139−(104+26)=9. Do not use corrected sodium to calculate these gaps.
- **Corrected Na = measured Na + 1.6 × ((glucose−100)/100).** ED: 130 + 1.6×5.84 = **139.34 mmol/L**. D1 0645: 135 + 1.6×3.24 = **140.18**. D1 1545: 138 + 1.6×0.84 = **139.34**. The initial measured hyponatremia largely reflects hyperglycemia; the measured Na rise during glucose correction is expected.
- **Winter's formula:** expected arterial pCO₂ = 1.5×HCO₃ + 8 ±2. ED: 1.5×9+8 = **21.5 ±2 mmHg** (19.5–23.5); measured **arterial** pCO₂ 22 supports appropriate respiratory compensation. Do not directly interpret later venous pCO₂ values against this arterial range.
- **Weight-based insulin:** 60 kg×0.1 = **6 units/hour**, or 6 mL/hour at 1 unit/mL. Once glucose <250: 60×0.05 = **3 units/hour**, or 3 mL/hour.
- **Glucose fall:** D1 0300→0400: 650−590=60 mg/dL/hour; 0400→0500=60; 0500→0600=60; 0600→0700=60; 0700→0800=60. During the potassium hold the fall slows. After restart 1000→1100=60 and 1100→1200=50. Near 200 on dextrose, the goal becomes safe glucose maintenance while clearing ketones, rather than continued 50–75 mg/dL/hour reduction.
- **Resolution at 1600:** specimen glucose 184 (<250 and <200), BHB 0.4 (<0.6), venous pH 7.37 (>7.3), HCO₃ 20 (≥18), gap 10 (≤12). This meets both the consensus endpoint and the requested glucose-plus-two checklist. Neither the 1145 bicarbonate of 16 nor a nearly closed gap permits early transition with BHB 1.6.
