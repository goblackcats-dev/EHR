## Judged acceptable but debatable (left as is)
1. **No potassium at K 3.6 (0900) although K fell 5.8 -> 3.6 in 7 hours.** The design (and many AKI practices) waits: the value is above the hold threshold, urine is borderline and creatinine is 3.3; the hospital medicine note says why and schedules the 1040 panel. A reader could argue for an earlier small bag; the design's trend (3.4 two hours later) is the teaching point.
2. **KCl bag 2 authorized at K 3.5 with insulin still held.** Two bags in 2 hours is the design's plan; the insulin hold continues until a confirmed 3.8. The total (20 mEq) is small for a patient who was 5.8 at arrival, which is the intended balance in AKI.
3. **D5NS 100 mL/h keeps running during the two riders (200 mL/h for 2 hours).** The series rule says to lower the main fluid while a rider runs if volume matters; here the design keeps it and the crackles at 1300 teach counting rider volume. The shift-1 IV total stays at the source's 900 mL.
4. **D10W (free water) 50 mL/h for 8 hours** while oliguric: Na 138-139 throughout and the order says to watch sodium; chosen by the source.
5. **Meal lispro 3 units and the bedtime correction 1 unit around the overlap with glargine 18 units.** Plausible and the cause of the 0645 low (a deliberate teaching event); the order set also says reduced renal clearance.
6. **Two-hour basal overlap, 2 hours of IV insulin after glargine at 1900** is the series standard (basal 1-2 h before stopping the drip).
7. **eGFR kept from the design with an unreliable-estimate label** rather than removed (the instructors asked to keep the column behavior).
8. **Results released at the design's 20-40 minute delays**, not the series standard (gas 15, BMP 45, BHB 55).
9. **Mild anemia (Hgb 12.3-12.4)** is explained by CKD and rehydration and not worked up; no bleeding is portrayed.
10. **Room SD-5, the mid-shift monitor values (0900, 1100, 1300, Fri 0645)** and the chart-only extras (lactate, calcium, albumin, PCR) are build choices.
