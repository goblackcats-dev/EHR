# SIMULATED PATIENT CHART — Marcus Reyes

**Fictional educational case.** All people, identifiers, events, orders, and results below are invented. Day 1 = November 5, 2026; Day 2 = November 6; Day 3 = November 7; Day 4 = November 8. Times are local, 24-hour clock. The simulation covers Day 1 0700–Day 4 0700: six 12-hour shifts and seven boundary checkpoints. The ED encounter begins at 0200 on Day 1, during the overnight period preceding unit arrival.

**Guideline reconciliation for faculty:** The requested “glucose <250 plus two findings” rule is displayed as a supplemental exercise. It is not the 2024 consensus definition and must not authorize stopping insulin. The actual resolution gate here is **beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB) <0.6 mmol/L AND either venous pH ≥7.3 or bicarbonate ≥18 mmol/L**, with clinical recovery; glucose ideally <200 mg/dL. This case meets both definitions at Day 1 1900. Initial IV insulin is 0.1 unit/kg/hour without a bolus; it decreases to 0.05 unit/kg/hour when glucose falls below 250, with dextrose added. These distinctions follow the [2024 ADA-led adult hyperglycemic crises consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11272983/).

Kidney-specific decisions are individualized for **AKI on CKD G3b**, not copied from an anuric dialysis protocol. Medication review, reassessment of volume status, and a documented plan for held medicines follow [KDIGO 2024 CKD principles](https://kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/KDIGO-2024-CKD-Guideline.pdf). AKI staging uses [KDIGO AKI criteria](https://kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/KDIGO-2012-AKI-Guideline-English.pdf). Lab ranges and operational protocols are fictional institutional specifications.

**Record conventions:** Only section 2 contains recorded vital signs, ending at unit arrival at 0700. All subsequent numeric cardiovascular, respiratory, oxygenation, and temperature values appear solely as faculty monitor presets in section 7. Sections 3–10 contain the future scenario; release each item only at its stated availability/event time. A sample time is not its result-availability time. “End: D4 0700” means the simulation/discharge boundary; unadministered contingency orders do not imply an event occurred.

**Calculation audit:**

- Anion gap = Na − (Cl + HCO₃); no potassium term. ED: 130 − (95 + 10) = **25**. D1 0700 release: 135 − (103 + 14) = **18**. D1 1500 release: 138 − (108 + 18) = **12**. D1 1900 release: 138 − (108 + 21) = **9**. Every populated gap in the table uses the same arithmetic; a gap of 7 later is a mildly low gap, not recurrent ketoacidosis.
- Corrected Na = Na + 1.6 × [(glucose − 100)/100]. ED: 130 + 1.6 × 5.20 = **138.3 mmol/L**. D1 0640 sample: 135 + 1.6 × 2.80 = **139.5**. D1 0840: 136 + 1.6 × 1.50 = **138.4**. D1 1840: 138 + 1.6 × 0.80 = **139.3**. Use measured Na for the anion gap. Do not interpret the expected measured Na rise as an automatic indication for hypotonic resuscitation.
- Winter’s formula estimates **arterial** compensatory pCO₂ = 1.5 × HCO₃ + 8 ±2. ED: 1.5 × 10 + 8 = **23 ±2 mmHg**; venous pCO₂ 25 is broadly compatible, acknowledging venous–arterial differences. At D1 0640: 1.5 × 14 + 8 = **29 ±2**, versus venous 30. A venous sample cannot exclude a small mixed respiratory disorder; an arterial gas is reserved for clinical concern and is not obtained in this stable scenario.
- Initial effective osmolality = 2 × 130 + 620/18 = **294.4 mOsm/kg**; estimated total osmolality = 294.4 + 64/2.8 = **317.3**. This is DKA, without a separate HHS phenotype.
- Insulin: 0.1 × 80 = **8 units/hour** initially; 0.05 × 80 = **4 units/hour** after glucose <250. From D1 0300–0900, 600 → 240 gives (600 − 240)/6 = **60 mg/dL/hour**. Once dextrose is added and ketosis is being cleared, the goal becomes maintaining approximately 150–200, not continuing that hourly fall.
- At D1 1100, the requested supplemental rule with a bicarbonate cutoff of 15 would already pass glucose, pH, and bicarbonate, yet BHB is still 2.4. At 1500, even a cutoff of 18 plus gap ≤12 would pass while BHB remains 1.0. **Neither time permits stopping the drip.** At 1900 glucose 180, pH 7.38, HCO₃ 21, gap 9, and BHB 0.4 satisfy both the requested exercise and consensus gate.
