- **Titrate to urine output 0.5 mL/kg/h = ~41 mL/h (goal 30-50 mL/h)**: if UOP below 30 mL/h for 2 consecutive hours, increase rate by 20-25%; if above 50 mL/h for 2 hours, decrease by 20-25%. Early UOP is low (hr 1: 28, hr 2: 24), rate goes up, then settles in range.
