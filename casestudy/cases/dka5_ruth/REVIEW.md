## Judged acceptable but debatable (left as is)
1. **Magnesium 1.6 at 0650 was not replaced until 1130** (design). The ICU H&P says it will be rechecked at 1100 and replaced if still low; some clinicians would give 2 g with the first potassium riders because low magnesium makes potassium hard to correct.
2. **Fixed 0.1 unit/kg/h with D10W (not lowered to 0.05 under glucose 250).** The 2024 consensus ordinarily lowers the rate with dextrose; the design keeps 6 units/h and adds dextrose (up to 150 mL/h). The order says to call the prescriber if glucose cannot be held in range and the instructor notes call it a deliberate departure.
3. **Glucose does not rise during the 2 h 15 min insulin hold** (168 -> 156 -> 154) with D10W at 50 mL/h. Explained in the notes by continuing SGLT2-inhibitor glycosuria and residual insulin effect; real glucose would more often drift up.
4. **Positive balance +3.6 L by Day 2 0700** (6% of body weight) with no weights supplied. Fever, glycosuria and the deficit make it plausible, and the lungs stay clear, but the instructor may want to portray a Day 2 weight.
5. **Oral amoxicillin for pyelonephritis with sepsis.** Oral beta-lactams are less effective than fluoroquinolones or TMP-SMX (both unavailable here: resistance, allergy), and many guidelines suggest 10-14 days when a beta-lactam is used; the design (and ID note) chooses 7 calendar days, with a reassessment clause. Kept because the clinical response is prompt and drainage is unobstructed.
6. **Culture speed** (prelim 16.7 h, ID 30.7 h, final 33.5 h) is faster than most laboratories.
7. **HbA1c released at 0130** with the first panel (design); point-of-care analyzers can do this.
8. **D10W 150 mL/h as free water** with sodium 138-140: tolerated; watch for hyponatremia in longer runs.
9. **A glargine dose of 12 units at 2300 on Day 1 and Day 2**, 24-26 h after her 2100 home dose: standard once-daily spacing; the 2100 vs 2300 shift is stated.
10. **Mid-shift monitor values** (0915, 1300, 2100, 2300, Fri 1400, Fri 2015, Sat 2100), room ICU-9, the Fri 2015 near-fall vitals, three-PIV removal times and the Hb/platelet fill-in are build choices.
