## At every checkpoint, ask yourself
1. What changed since last time (vitals, exam, labs, orders, notes)? What is the trend?
2. What is the most dangerous problem right now (airway, breathing, heart, then the rest)?
3. What can I do now, what do I need an order for, and who do I call? Write your SBAR.
4. What could my next action harm (drug, fluid, line, protocol)? What must I NOT give?
5. What will I teach the patient and family?

## Prompts for each checkpoint
- **0700:** Which findings suggest an electrolyte problem? What exactly will you ask the wife? Which home medicines are not safe tonight, and which orders do you want clarified?
- **1100:** Which numbers worry you most? Whom do you call and what do you say (write SBAR)? What do you prepare at the bedside?
- **1500:** What are the thresholds for calling the ICU? What must you check before dialysis starts? What can and can not be done with the dialysis catheter?
- **1900:** What trend did you miss or catch? How will you treat the low glucose safely? What will you watch for after dialysis?
- **2300:** What are the possible causes of new confusion? What will you do instead of giving a sleep medicine? How will you protect his lines?
- **0300:** What do the last three magnesium levels tell you? What will you do about his constipation, and what will you not give? Whom do you call?
- **0700:** What do you want to teach, and how will you check that he and his wife understood? What will you hand off to the day nurse?
