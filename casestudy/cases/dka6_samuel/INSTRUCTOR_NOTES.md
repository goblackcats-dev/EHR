## Teaching points
1. **Diabetes can hide a heart attack**: epigastric ache, "indigestion" or unusual fatigue with sweating can be ischemia; DKA can be the first sign. Check the ECG and troponin in every DKA of unclear cause.
2. **DKA resolves on ketones and acid-base**, not the glucose or the gap: BHB under 0.6 plus pH 7.3 or higher or bicarbonate 18 or higher; give basal insulin 2 hours before stopping the drip. Never hold basal insulin for NPO.
3. **Potassium on insulin in an ischemic heart**: K under 3.5 means hold, replace, restart at 3.5 or higher with the prescriber; goal 4.0-5.0. Peripheral limit 10 mEq/h from ALL sources: a 20 mEq/L carrier plus a rider breaks it, so swap to a potassium-free carrier first. PVCs, flat T and U waves are the signs; a normal ECG does not exclude danger.
4. **Fluids in DKA with an acute MI**: a liter in the first hour, then at or under 150 mL/h in total (riders count); lungs, urine and weight watched.
5. **Heparin math**: 60 units/kg bolus capped at 4,000; 12 units/kg/h capped at 1,000; 80 kg -> 4,000 and 960 units/h (9.6 mL/h); anti-Xa 0.30-0.70; platelets; stop for the cath lab.
6. **Nitroglycerin safety**: SBP 100 or higher, no PDE-5 inhibitor in 48 hours, sit down, ECG with the first episode, call if not relieved 5 minutes after the first dose.
7. **Beta-blocker parameters** and hypoglycemia masking (sweating persists); glucose before driving; glucose tablets in the cab.
8. **Cath and stent nursing**: pre-procedure checklist, contrast-kidney hydration, radial band deflation, neurovascular checks, right-arm restrictions, bleeding precautions on aspirin + ticagrelor; the radial hematoma (pressure, mark, check, teach, do not stop DAPT).
9. **DAPT adherence and cost**: never stop ticagrelor early; copay assistance; schedule 12 hours apart.
10. **Contrast timing**: creatinine 19 h and 43 h after the contrast here; injury would show as a rise of 0.3 or more within 48 h; repeat at the 1-week visit; ARB started with K and creatinine checks.
11. **Tobacco and the driver license**: nicotine replacement after a stent is much safer than smoking; withdrawal at 52 hours; certification pathway with weeks of leave, cardiology clearance (LVEF 55%, exercise test) and an endocrinology form.
12. **Sick-day rules** (never omit basal; ketones and glucose every 2-4 hours; fluids; emergency triggers including any chest or epigastric discomfort).
13. **VTE prophylaxis follows risk**: none here (therapeutic heparin, then DAPT, ambulatory); contrast with Ruth (dka5).
