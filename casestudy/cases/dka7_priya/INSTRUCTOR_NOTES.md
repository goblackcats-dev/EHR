## Teaching points
1. **DKA in pregnancy occurs at lower glucose** (placental glucose use, hormone resistance, vomiting, lower renal threshold, less buffer): check ketones, not just glucose, in any ill pregnant person with type 1 diabetes.
2. **Pregnancy blood gas**: baseline HCO3 18-22 and pCO2 27-32; compare with Winter's formula but expect a lower pCO2; pH from Henderson-Hasselbalch must fit.
3. **Category II tracing in maternal DKA**: left lateral, fluids, insulin with dextrose, potassium; no emergent cesarean; reassess hourly; expect Category I as the acidosis corrects (here at about 1100).
4. **DKA resolves on ketones and acid-base**, not the glucose; glargine 2 hours before the drip stops; never hold basal insulin.
5. **Potassium on insulin**: under 3.5 hold, replace, restart at 3.5+ with the prescriber; peripheral limit 10 mEq/h from ALL sources; swap to a potassium-free carrier first; **keep the dextrose during the hold** (fetal substrate).
6. **Magnesium 2 g** for refractory hypokalemia: reflexes, respirations, urine output (not the preeclampsia load).
7. **Hypoglycemia after a stomach virus**: depleted glycogen, higher sensitivity; Rule of 15; reduce basal 17%.
8. **Pregnancy targets**: fasting under 95, 1 hour under 140, 2 hours under 120.
9. **Preterm contractions**: speculum swab for fFN before any digital exam; cervical length 3.0 cm or longer reassuring; fFN valid 24-34 weeks only; hydration and rest; **betamethasone raises glucose for days** (30-40% insulin increase or infusion if ever given).
10. **GBS not yet due; penicillin rash is low risk (cefazolin); Rh positive: no RhIG.**
11. **Ondansetron** is acceptable in the third trimester but prolongs QT: hold while K or Mg is low.
12. **VTE reasoning in pregnancy** (SCDs, walking, no drug here), and aspirin 81 mg is for preeclampsia prevention.
13. **Discharge teaching**: kick counts, warning signs (preeclampsia, preterm labor, ketones), sick-day rules, CGM verification, glucagon.
